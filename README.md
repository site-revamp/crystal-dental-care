# Crystal Dental Care — Homepage Redesign

## Website Overview

Crystal Dental Care is a Singapore-based dental clinic located in Serangoon, providing general, cosmetic, restorative, and specialist dental treatments. Its services include preventive dentistry, orthodontics, dental implants, root canal treatment, oral surgery, and smile design.

The redesigned homepage focuses on creating a modern, welcoming, and patient-centred digital experience that communicates the clinic's services more clearly, highlights its clinical capabilities, and makes appointment enquiries more accessible.

## Main Improvements

### 1. Stronger First Impression and Appointment Guidance

**Previous Issue:** The original homepage headline communicated the clinic's philosophy but did not sufficiently guide visitors towards exploring relevant treatments or booking an appointment.

**Redesign Improvement:** Strengthened the homepage introduction with clearer patient-focused messaging, supporting information, and prominent treatment exploration and appointment actions. This establishes a more direct journey from the initial impression to the next step.

### 2. Improved Treatment Discovery and Service Explanations

**Previous Issue:** The original service presentation relied heavily on short treatment labels and imagery, providing limited context about individual procedures and the dental concerns they address.

**Redesign Improvement:** Organised key dental treatments into a clearer, more informative service presentation featuring descriptive content and treatment navigation. Visitors can better understand available options and identify services relevant to their dental needs without unnecessary browsing.

### 3. Stronger Integration of Trust and Clinic Information

**Previous Issue:** Important trust-building information, including the dental team, clinical facilities, hygiene practices, and contact details, lacked a sufficiently connected presentation within the homepage experience.

**Redesign Improvement:** Brought clinician information, modern dental facilities, the clinic's hygiene approach, and contact pathways into a more cohesive homepage structure. The updated presentation helps visitors become familiar with the practice and locate relevant information before making an appointment enquiry.

## Design Improvements

The homepage redesign focuses on several targeted visual and usability enhancements:

- **Clearer Visual Hierarchy:** Established a more structured progression from the main introduction to treatments, clinical information, and appointment opportunities.
- **Improved Service Presentation:** Added clearer treatment explanations and more accessible navigation to support informed service discovery.
- **Modern, Welcoming Appearance:** Emphasised clean layouts, balanced spacing, and a professional visual presentation consistent with the clinic's positioning.
- **Enhanced Trust Communication:** Increased the prominence of dental team information, clinical facilities, and hygiene-related content.
- **Improved Appointment Accessibility:** Prioritised clear booking and contact actions throughout the patient journey.
- **Consistent Content Organisation:** Created a more cohesive presentation of treatment information, practice details, and supporting visual elements.

## Summary

The redesigned Crystal Dental Care homepage addresses three primary communication and usability challenges: insufficiently conversion-focused first impressions, limited treatment explanations, and fragmented trust-building information.

The redesign establishes a clearer, more informative, and patient-focused experience that supports treatment discovery, communicates the clinic's capabilities, and provides more direct pathways towards appointment enquiries.
